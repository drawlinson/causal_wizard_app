--- title: "Pending Results Queue" summary: "You cannot request new results while others are still processing." keywords: "results, pending, queue, processing" categorySlugs: ["causal-wizard-concept", "process"] ---

To fairly share the computational load on our servers, we prevent any one user from requesting an excessive number of Results at the same time.

When you have too many pending Results, you will not be able to request additional Results until the pending jobs have completed.

Solution: Just wait a while.

I've been waiting for too long

If you've been waiting for more than a day, contact us. Something might have gone wrong and we'll fix it.
